# E-Commerce Product Analytics

## Project Brief
End-to-end product analytics case study on a real e-commerce dataset — 
simulating the work of a Product Analyst joining a company and 
building their first insights from scratch.

**Dataset:** Online Retail II (UCI Machine Learning Repository)  
**Tools:** Python, Pandas, SQL, Matplotlib, Seaborn  
**Status:** In progress

---

## Business Questions This Project Answers

1. How is revenue and MAU trending month-on-month?
2. What is our retention rate — are customers coming back?
3. Where are users dropping off, and why?
4. What are the top recommendations to improve retention?
